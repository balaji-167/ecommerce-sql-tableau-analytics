# Tableau Dashboard Guide

## Overview

This Tableau workbook contains four dashboards built from SQL-generated Gold layer analytical outputs.

The dashboards are designed to help analyze business performance across executive KPIs, product profitability, customer health, churn risk, customer behaviour, funnel conversion, and retention.

Interactive Tableau Public dashboard:

[View Tableau Public Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/shared/92S6K28XY?:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

---

## Dashboard 1: Executive Overview

### Purpose

The Executive Overview dashboard provides a high-level summary of overall business performance.

### Main Components

- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Median Order Value
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Country
- Global Revenue Distribution

### How to Read This Dashboard

Use the KPI cards at the top to understand overall business scale.  
Use the monthly revenue trend to identify high and low revenue periods.  
Use the country bar chart and global map to understand geographic revenue contribution.

### Business Questions Answered

- How much revenue did the business generate?
- How many orders and customers were recorded?
- What is the typical order value?
- Which countries contribute the most revenue?
- Is revenue stable, growing, or fluctuating over time?

## Dashboard 2: Product & Category Performance

### Purpose

The Product & Category Performance dashboard analyzes product-level and category-level revenue, profit, margin, and rating performance.

### Main Components

- Top Products by Revenue
- Top Products by Profit
- Category Revenue vs Profit
- Category Profit Margin
- Product Rating Segment Distribution
- Product Profitability Matrix

### How to Read This Dashboard

Use the top product charts to identify the highest revenue and highest profit products.  
Use the category charts to compare revenue, profit, and margin efficiency.  
Use the profitability matrix to identify high-performing products and low-contribution products.  
Use the rating distribution to understand product quality perception.

### Business Questions Answered

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which categories have the best margins?
- Are high-revenue products also high-profit products?
- Which products need attention based on profitability and ratings?

## Dashboard 3: Customer Health & Retention

### Purpose

The Customer Health & Retention dashboard identifies customer churn risk, revenue at risk, customer lifetime value, RFM segmentation, and recovery opportunities.

### Main Components

- Total Customers
- Active Customers
- At Risk / Dormant Customers
- Revenue at Risk
- Average Lifetime Revenue
- Customer Churn Risk Funnel
- Revenue at Churn Risk
- Top Customer Recovery Targets
- Customer Lifetime Value Matrix
- RFM Value Segment Matrix
- 12-Month Customer Retention Curve

### How to Read This Dashboard

Use the KPI cards to understand customer base health.  
Use the churn risk funnel and revenue-at-risk chart to identify customer loss risk.  
Use the recovery target chart to prioritize customers for win-back campaigns.  
Use the RFM matrix to understand customer value distribution.  
Use the retention curve to understand repeat customer behaviour after first purchase.

### Business Questions Answered

- How many customers are active, at risk, dormant, or lost?
- How much revenue is at risk?
- Which customers should be targeted for recovery?
- How are customers distributed by RFM value?
- How does retention behave after first purchase?

## Dashboard 4: Customer Behaviour & Retention Analytics

### Purpose

The Customer Behaviour & Retention Analytics dashboard focuses on user journey behaviour, funnel conversion, source-device performance, path transitions, and recent cohort retention.

### Main Components

- Source & Device Purchase Conversion
- Session Funnel Conversion
- Top User Path Transitions
- Customer Retention Heatmap — Last 24 Months

### How to Read This Dashboard

Use the source-device heatmap to identify which traffic source and device combinations convert best.  
Use the funnel chart to understand where users drop from page view to purchase.  
Use the user path chart to identify the most common customer journey transitions.  
Use the retention heatmap to compare repeat behaviour across recent customer cohorts.

### Business Questions Answered

- Which source-device combinations convert best?
- Where do users drop off in the funnel?
- What are the most common user journey transitions?
- How does retention change across recent cohorts?
- Which parts of the customer journey need improvement?

## Dashboard Design Approach

The dashboards were designed with:

- Clean executive layout
- KPI cards for quick performance monitoring
- Heatmaps for conversion and retention comparison
- Bar charts for ranking and funnel analysis
- Scatter/matrix visuals for customer and product segmentation
- Consistent business-focused tooltips
- Tableau Public sharing for interactive exploration
